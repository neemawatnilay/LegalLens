
IN THE FAMILY COURT AT BANGALORE
Petition No. ___ / 2023

PETITION UNDER SECTION 13 OF THE HINDU MARRIAGE ACT, 1955
FOR DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE BY DECREE OF DIVORCE

PETITIONER: Smt. Priya Sharma, aged 32 years
RESPONDENT: Sri. Rahul Sharma, aged 35 years

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. That the Petitioner and Respondent were married on 15th March 2018
at Bangalore as per Hindu rites and customs. The marriage was
registered at the Sub-Registrar Office, Koramangala, Bangalore
on 20th March 2018. The marriage invitation card bearing the
venue as The Leela Palace, Bangalore is annexed hereto as
Exhibit A.

2. That after the marriage, the couple resided at #45, 3rd Cross,
Jayanagar, Bangalore - 560041, which is the self-acquired
property of the Respondent's father Sri. Mohan Sharma.
The Petitioner contributed Rs. 3,00,000 towards interior
furnishing of the said property from her personal savings.

3. That the Petitioner was employed as a Senior Software Engineer
at Infosys Limited, Electronic City, Bangalore drawing a
monthly salary of Rs. 85,000 at the time of marriage. She
resigned from her employment in August 2018 at the insistence
of the Respondent who claimed that both spouses working
created social discomfort in his family.

4. That from September 2018, the Respondent's mother Smt. Kamala
Sharma began making persistent demands that the Petitioner's
parents provide a Maruti Swift Dzire car as the same was
promised during pre-marriage negotiations. The Petitioner's
father Sri. Venkatesh Rao categorically denies any such
promise was made during the engagement ceremony held on
12th November 2017 at Hotel Taj Vivanta, Mysore.

5. That on the intervening night of 3rd and 4th January 2019,
the Respondent in an inebriated condition subjected the
Petitioner to severe physical assault causing injuries to
her left arm and shoulder. The Petitioner was treated at
Columbia Asia Hospital, Hebbal on 4th January 2019 and
the medical records are annexed as Exhibit B. The treating
doctor Dr. Anil Kumar noted contusions on the right arm
and upper back in his examination report.

6. That the Respondent is employed as a Project Manager at
Wipro Technologies, Sarjapur Road, Bangalore and draws
a monthly compensation of Rs. 2,20,000 inclusive of all
allowances as per his latest salary slip dated October 2022
annexed as Exhibit C. The Respondent had represented to
the Petitioner's family during matrimonial negotiations
that his monthly income was Rs. 1,80,000.

7. That following the assault, the Petitioner lodged a
complaint at the Jayanagar Police Station on 5th January
2019 bearing complaint number JYN/0045/2019. The police
after inquiry filed a report stating the matter was of
civil nature and no FIR was registered. The said complaint
acknowledgment is annexed as Exhibit D.

8. That the Petitioner's parents visited the matrimonial home
on 10th January 2019 to attempt reconciliation in the
presence of community elders including Sri. Ramesh Gowda
and Sri. Prakash Hegde. During this meeting the Respondent
agreed in writing to refrain from any further demands and
to maintain peaceful cohabitation. The said written
undertaking dated 10th January 2019 is annexed as Exhibit E.

9. That despite the undertaking, the Respondent's behavior
did not improve. On 2nd February 2019, the Respondent
locked the Petitioner out of the matrimonial home while
she had gone to visit her parents in Mysore for the weekend.
The Petitioner's personal belongings including her
educational certificates, jewelry valued at approximately
Rs. 8,50,000 and electronic items remain in the matrimonial
home till date.

10. That the Petitioner is currently residing with her parents
at #23, Vivekananda Nagar, Mysore - 570023. The Petitioner
has been residing separately from the Respondent since
2nd February 2019, a period of approximately four years
at the time of filing this petition.

11. That the Respondent has contracted a second marriage with
one Ms. Deepika Nair on 14th August 2022 at Thiruvananthapuram
as per reliable information received by the Petitioner
through common acquaintances. The Petitioner has annexed
a photograph of the said ceremony as Exhibit F obtained
through social media.

12. That the Petitioner has a minor daughter aged 3 years born
on 18th September 2019 from the said wedlock who is
presently in her custody and care. The child has been
enrolled in Poorna Prajna School, Mysore for her primary
education.

13. That the Respondent has not paid any maintenance or
provided any financial support to the Petitioner or the
child since February 2019 despite having sufficient means.
The Respondent however transferred Rs. 15,000 to the
Petitioner's bank account on 22nd March 2022 with the
remark 'child expenses' as evidenced by the bank statement
annexed as Exhibit G.

14. That the Petitioner states that she has made genuine efforts
towards reconciliation and approached the Respondent
through mutual friends in mid 2020. The Respondent refused
all attempts at reconciliation and stated that the marriage
was irretrievably broken.

15. That the cause of action for filing this petition arose
at Bangalore, within the jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court
where the parties last resided together.

PRAYER:
The Petitioner therefore prays that this Hon'ble Court may
be pleased to:
a) Pass a decree of divorce dissolving the marriage between
   the Petitioner and the Respondent
b) Grant permanent custody of the minor child to the Petitioner
c) Direct the Respondent to pay maintenance of Rs. 40,000
   per month to the Petitioner and child
d) Direct the Respondent to return the Petitioner's belongings
   and jewelry from the matrimonial home
e) Pass such other orders as this Hon'ble Court deems fit

Verified at Bangalore on this 15th day of January 2023

Petitioner
Smt. Priya Sharma
